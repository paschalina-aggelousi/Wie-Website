IEEE UoM Women In Engineering

Dedicated to empowering and advancing women in technology, the IEEE UoM Women In Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group at the University of Macedonia, founded in 2026, is a vibrant community connected with the global IEEE WIE network. Our mission is centered on uplifting women in electrical engineering, computer science and related STEM fields by creating strong opportunities for professional growth, technical excellence and innovation.

About us

What We Do

We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and career execution through targeted initiatives designed to support women in tech:

Hands-on Workshops & Tech Talks

Gaining practical experience with emerging tools, industry insights and specialized technologies.

Mentorship & Career Development

Connecting members with experienced professionals, alumni and peers to guide their career paths.

Community Outreach & Impact

Using technology as a vehicle for positive social change and encouraging younger generations of girls to explore STEM fields.

Leadership Opportunities

Empowering members to take initiative, manage projects and refine essential leadership skills.

Why Join Us?

Being a part of the IEEE UoM WIE Affinity Group gives you access to a global network of innovators. Members gain:

Professional Networking

Direct contact with industry experts, research pioneers and supportive mentors.

Skill Building

Access to specialized resources, educational programs and technical project teams.

Exclusive Access

Opportunities to participate in global IEEE events, conferences and competitions.

Our Vision

We aim to build a strong, inclusive ecosystem at the University of Macedonia where female engineers and tech enthusiasts build confidence, showcase their potential and lead the future of technology.
