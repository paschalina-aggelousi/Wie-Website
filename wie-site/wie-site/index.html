News

October 2026

IEEE WIE at the University of Macedonia: A Student-Led Initiative

The IEEE Women in Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group at the University of Macedonia marks the beginning of a new student-led initiative focused on engineering, technology, collaboration, and inclusion.

The establishment of the IEEE Women In Engineering (WIE) Student Branch Affinity Group at the University of Macedonia marks the beginning of a new student-led initiative focused on engineering, technology, collaboration, and inclusion.

Founded through the initiative of seven female students, the group aims to foster a supportive community where students can exchange ideas, develop their skills, and explore opportunities for academic and professional growth.

Through events, educational activities, networking opportunities, and collaborations with academic and industry professionals, IEEE WIE seeks to encourage engagement with engineering and technology while promoting an inclusive environment in which students can learn, connect, and grow together.

As we begin this journey, our goal is to build a strong and active community that creates meaningful opportunities for students and contributes to a more inclusive future in engineering and technology. We invite students from all backgrounds to join us in shaping the future of engineering and technology at the University of Macedonia.

About us

What We Do

We bridge the gap between academic knowledge and career execution through targeted initiatives designed to support women in tech:

Hands-on Workshops & Tech Talks

Gaining practical experience with emerging tools, industry insights and specialized technologies.

Mentorship & Career Development

Connecting members with experienced professionals, alumni and peers to guide their career paths.

Community Outreach & Impact

Using technology as a vehicle for positive social change and encouraging younger generations of girls to explore STEM fields.

Leadership Opportunities

Empowering members to take initiative, manage projects and refine essential leadership skills.

Subteams

Our group is organized into three subteams.

Fundraising & HR

Secures sponsorships and funding for our events and projects, and looks after our members: recruiting, welcoming newcomers and keeping the team motivated and connected.

Social Media & Graphic Design

Gives the group its look and its voice: designing posters and event material, running our social media accounts and promoting our activities to the community.

Project Making

Turns ideas into real projects: planning and building hands-on technical initiatives, workshops and activities, and learning to work as a team from the first idea to the final result.

Why Join Us?

Being a part of the IEEE UoM WIE Affinity Group gives you access to a global network of innovators. Members gain:

Professional Networking

Direct contact with industry experts, research pioneers and supportive mentors.

Skill Building

Access to specialized resources, educational programs and technical project teams.

Exclusive Access

Opportunities to participate in global IEEE events, conferences and competitions.

Our Vision

We aim to build a strong, inclusive ecosystem at the University of Macedonia where female engineers and tech enthusiasts build confidence, showcase their potential and lead the future of technology.

FAQs

How can I join the IEEE WIE team at the University of Macedonia?

Fill out our Join Us form to express your interest in becoming part of our community!

Is IEEE WIE only for women?

IEEE WIE welcomes individuals of all genders and backgrounds who share an interest in technology and engineering.

Does IEEE WIE membership cost anything?

IEEE WIE membership costs US$25 per year and is free to IEEE Student, Graduate Student, and Life Members.

How much time does it take to participate?

Participation is flexible! You can choose how much time to dedicate based on your availability and interests.

Do I need experience in technology to participate?

No! Everyone interested in technology, engineering, and STEM is welcome. You don't need previous experience, just curiosity and a willingness to learn.

How can I contact you?

Feel free to email us or send us a message on social media.
